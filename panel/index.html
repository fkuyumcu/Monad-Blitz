1 Wallet Connected

Paste the private key of the wallet you funded from the faucet (MetaMask → Account details → Show private key). The key stays in this browser and is never sent to a server. Use a testnet wallet only.

2 Contract Connected

Deploy a new contract or enter the address of one you deployed before.

3 Bot reviewers Running

When a job is submitted, 3 reviewers are picked from the pool, so the pool needs at least 3. Bots run in this tab: they stake, score, seal, reveal and advance jobs whose time is up. Keep this tab open during the demo. To test with your own phone, turn one bot off. With 2 bots and you in the pool, the task always goes to you.
