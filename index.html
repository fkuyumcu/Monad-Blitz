Consilio

Three independent reviewers score each freelance delivery. The median score sets the payment.

Open live screen View contract

How a job gets settled

  1. ClientLocks the payment in the contract. 10% of it is set aside as the review fee.
  2. FreelancerSubmits the work. The contract picks three reviewers at random from a staked pool.
  3. ReviewersScore from 1 to 10 without seeing each other's scores. Votes are sealed first, then revealed together.
  4. ContractComputes the median. Pays out the payment, the fee and the penalties.

Example: a real AI training data task

Voice editing session

A contributor selects text on screen, gives a spoken command in Turkish, and an LLM rewrites only the selected text. They submit the audio, the transcript and a short log.

  1. Audio file and transcript are included
  2. The selected text is quoted
  3. Technical terms and meaning are kept
  4. Output is checked and adds no new facts
  5. Cursor position and task metadata are filled in
Command: "seçili cümleyi daha resmi yap"
Selected text: "API token süresi dolunca 401 dönüyor."
LLM output: "API token süresi dolduğunda sunucu 401 yanıtı döndürür ve 30 saniye sonra yeniden dener."
6Reviewer I
6Reviewer II
10Reviewer III
6Median

The output added a retry rule that was not in the selected text, and the cursor and metadata fields are missing. Two reviewers caught it and gave 6. The reviewer who gave 10 without checking loses part of their stake. The freelancer gets 60% of the payment.

Rules

8+

Full payment. Below a median of 8, the freelancer is paid in proportion and the rest goes back to the client.

±3

Penalty line. A reviewer who is 3 or more points off the median, or who does not vote or reveal, loses part of their stake.

3-2-1

Fee share. An exact match gets 3 shares, 1 point off gets 2, 2 points off gets 1. The fee and penalties are split by these shares.
